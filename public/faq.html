How EscrowCourt works

1. Propose & deploy

The buyer defines milestones — a description and a GEN amount for each — and deploys a dedicated Deal contract naming the freelancer. This transaction is signed by the buyer's own wallet; nothing is deployed by a server.

2. Fund

The buyer sends the exact sum of all milestone amounts to the contract. Funds are now locked in escrow, milestone by milestone.

3. Submit & auto-review

The freelancer submits a description of the completed work for a milestone. Anyone can then trigger a review: a GenLayer validator leader compares the deliverable against the milestone's requirement, other validators independently re-check, and the network only accepts a verdict — approved or rejected — once they agree.

4. Dispute, if needed

If a milestone is rejected and either side disagrees, both the buyer and the freelancer can submit a written statement. Once ready, anyone can trigger the final ruling: an AI arbitrator weighs both statements independently of the earlier automated rejection and issues a binding decision — approved or refunded. There is exactly one dispute round per milestone.

5. Claim

Once a milestone is approved, the freelancer claims that milestone's payment. If it's refunded, the buyer claims it back. Each milestone is fully independent — work continues on the others regardless of one milestone's outcome.
